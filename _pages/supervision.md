### PhD scholarship: quantum machine learning for medical imaging (Quantum Motion & Monash University) {#phd-scholarship}

I am recruiting a PhD student for [Quantum-Enhanced Machine Learning Models for Digital Health and Medical Diagnostics](https://careers.pageuppeople.com/513/cw/en/job/701336/phd-scholarship-opportunity-quantumenhanced-machine-learning-models-for-digital-health-and-medical-diagnostics), a joint project between Quantum Motion and Monash University. Come join a world-leading university research group, [Prof. Muhammad Usman](https://au.linkedin.com/in/usman63)'s Scalable Quantum Systems Lab at Monash, and a pioneering quantum computing company for a project at the frontier of emerging quantum applications. Prof. Usman and I will supervise the project jointly, so you get the best of both academia and industry while earning your PhD in Melbourne, consistently rated among the world's most liveable cities.

**Who should apply.** The scholarship is open to international and domestic students with a strong background in quantum computing and/or machine learning. You should have a four-year bachelor's or a master's degree that includes a research project, with First Class Honours or equivalent grades.

**Scholarship.** A full scholarship of about $41,100 AUD per year (2027 rate, indexed annually) for up to 3.5 years, plus a tuition fee scholarship for international students, candidature funding and a travel grant.

**How to apply.** [Full details and contact addresses are in the advert](https://careers.pageuppeople.com/513/cw/en/job/701336/phd-scholarship-opportunity-quantumenhanced-machine-learning-models-for-digital-health-and-medical-diagnostics).

**How to apply.** Applications close on 21 January 2027 at 18:00 GMT, and interviews run on a rolling basis. [Full details are in the advert](https://quantummotion.jobs.personio.com/job/2853487).
